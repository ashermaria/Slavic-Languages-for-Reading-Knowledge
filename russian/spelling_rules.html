Spelling Rules Background

Spelling rules are a set of grammatical constraints that determine what vowels can follow what consonants. There are three "irregular" consonant groupings:

Group of 4 - Ж, Ч, Ш, Щ (used in every rule)

Group of 3 - Г, К, Х

Group of 1 - Ц

There are three spelling rules based on these groupings:

1 - 8-Letter Spelling Rule (Groups 4, 3, & 1)

After Г, К, Х, Ж, Ч, Ш, Щ + Ц

if you would normally have Я or Ю, always А or У (respectively) instead

2 - 7-letter Spelling Rule (Groups 4 & 3)

After Ж, Ч, Ш, Щ, Г, К, Х

if you would normally have an Ы, put И instead

3 - 5-Letter Spelling Rule (Groups 4 & 1)

After Ж, Ч, Ш, Щ + Ц

if О, always stressed

if Е, always unstressed

Examples

Могу (not Могю)

Кошкы (not Кошки)

Лицо (litsO, not lItso

Пляжи (not Пляжы)

Шедевр (shedYEvr, not shYEdever)
