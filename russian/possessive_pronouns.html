Pronoun Background

In Russian, pronouns change based on number, gender, plurality, and case. You do not need to memorize this list yet, but it is put here so that you have it as an easily accessible reference. In the 3rd person, the choice of pronoun is determined by the gender of the noun referred to.

Pronoun Table

Case 1st person singular 2nd person singular 3rd person singular (m/f/n) 1st person plural 2nd person plural 3rd person plural
Nominative я ты он/она/оно мы вы они
Accusative меня тебя его/её/его нас вас их
Genitive меня тебя его/её/его нас вас их
Dative мне тебе ему/ей/ему нам вам им
Prepositional мне тебе нём/ней/нём нас вас них
Instrumental мной тобой ним/ней/ним нами вами ними

In the accusative, genitive and dative cases, the third-person pronouns will sometimes have an н- in front of them, which is a variant used after the majority of prepositions as to not have two vowel sounds in a row. For example его becomes него
