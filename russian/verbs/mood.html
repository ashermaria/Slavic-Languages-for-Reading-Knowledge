Introduction to Mood

In Russian, verbs (almost) always have two aspects (вид): imperfective (несовершенный) and perfective (совершенный). Aspect is simply a way of describing an action, not a tense.

Imperfective actions are processes; close-up view where beginning, end, result, and cause do not matter.

Perfective actions are isolated events; bird-eye view where you describe the action in its entirety: beginning, middle, end.

Both imperfective and perfective infinitives can be used after verbs of any tense. The aspect pairs are highly irregular must be memorized.

Tense uses:

1. present-tense conjugated imperfective verbs are present tense

2. present-tense conjugated perfective verbs are, confusingly, future tense

3. both can be used in the past tense

Examples of aspect difference:

читать → прочитать

прогуливать → прогулять

брать → взять

говорить → сказать
