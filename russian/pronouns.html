Pronoun Background

In Russian, pronouns change based on number, gender, plurality, and case. You do not need to memorize this list yet, but it is put here so that you have it as an easily accessible reference. In the 3rd person, the choice of pronoun is determined by the gender of the noun referred to. For the pronouns that have the н- in front of them, that variant is only used after the majority of prepositions. Otherwise, you use the plain form.
