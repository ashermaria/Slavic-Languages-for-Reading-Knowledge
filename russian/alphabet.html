Russian uses the Russian Cyrillic alphabet. The alphabet has 33 letters. Many of the letters function similarly to English, so being able to read in Latin is already an advantage.

As you will notice, many of the letters are the same in English. A, E, K, M, O, and T are (largely) pronounced the same.

The rest of the letters are either new pronunciations of familiar Latin letters like B, H, P, C, and Y, or are new shapes altogether.

In the table, you can see a detailed list of the letters and examples of their pronunciation.

Russian vowel are either stressed or unstressed depending on the word. Every word has one stressed vowel*

*If a word only has one vowel, it is almost always stressed.
