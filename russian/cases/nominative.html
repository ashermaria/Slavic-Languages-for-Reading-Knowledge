Nominative Case

The nominative case gets its name from the concept of "naming" (Latin nōminātīvus (“pertaining to naming, nominative”)). Therefore, this is the case of the grammatical subject

Consonant Rules

In order to determine how plurals are constructed in Russian, we need to first go over one more spelling rule

All consonants in Russian are either hard or soft, which produce different sounds

A consonant is HARD if followed by the following, А, О, У, Ы, Э, Ъ, another consonant, or nothing

A consonant is SOFT if followed by the following, Я, Ё, Ю, И, Е, Ь, or Й (depends)

Examples

том - hard

столь - soft
